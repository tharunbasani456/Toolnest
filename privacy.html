Privacy Policy

This page explains the limited information that may be handled when you visit ToolNest.

Last updated: 1 November 2026

Overview

ToolNest is a website for discovering digital tools and reading practical guides. We aim to collect as little personal information as reasonably possible. This policy describes the website's current intended operation; it will be updated if we add features or services that change how information is handled.

Information you provide

The current website does not ask you to create an account or submit personal information to browse its pages. If you contact us through an external service or send information through a channel you choose, that service may process the information you provide under its own privacy policy.

Cookies, analytics and advertising

ToolNest does not currently claim to operate its own analytics, advertising or user-tracking programme. The website may load fonts from Google Fonts, which can involve a request to Google's servers. The hosting provider may also process technical request data as part of delivering and securing the website.

If analytics, advertising technologies, consent tools or other tracking features are introduced, this policy will be revised to explain them and any choices available to visitors.

Links to other websites

Our pages may link to third-party websites, including software providers and learning platforms. We do not control those websites. Their privacy practices, cookies, terms and data collection are governed by their own policies. Please review those policies before sharing information or creating an account.

Data security and retention

We do not currently provide a ToolNest account system or a form that collects visitor submissions. Website hosting and infrastructure providers may retain technical logs for operational, security or legal purposes under their own policies.

Children

ToolNest is a general information website and is not designed to knowingly collect personal information from children. Parents or guardians should supervise children's use of online services and review the privacy terms of any third-party product.

Your choices and questions

If you have a privacy question or believe information about you has been sent to us, please contact us through the channel on our Contact page and explain the request. Applicable privacy rights depend on the laws that apply to your circumstances.

Policy updates

We may update this policy when the website changes. The date at the top of this page indicates the latest revision. Please check this page periodically.

This page is a plain-language summary, not legal advice. Before enabling advertising, analytics, user accounts or collecting contact-form submissions, the policy and website practices should be reviewed for the laws and platform rules that apply.
