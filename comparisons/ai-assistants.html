ChatGPT vs Gemini vs Claude: which AI assistant should you choose?

A neutral, use-case-led comparison of three popular AI assistants, using their official documentation rather than unsupported benchmark claims.

Independent editorial comparison · Checked November 2026 · Features and availability can change

Quick answer

There is no universal winner. Choose based on the work you need to do, the tools you already use, and how well each assistant handles your own examples. ChatGPT, Gemini and Claude overlap, but their current feature sets and integrations are not identical.

What the official product documentation says

ChatGPT

OpenAI describes capabilities including question answering, drafting, reasoning, web search, deep research, image input and generation, file uploads, data analysis and Projects. Some tools and limits depend on plan and settings. See the official capabilities overview.

Google Gemini

Google documents Deep Research that can use Google Search and, when connected and permitted, sources such as Gmail and Drive. Gemini also supports uploading and analysing documents, spreadsheets, images, videos and other files, subject to limits. See Gemini Deep Research and file uploads and analysis.

Claude

Anthropic documents file creation and editing for formats such as spreadsheets, presentations, documents and PDFs. Claude Code also supports shareable interactive Artifacts for certain development workflows. Check the current Claude file creation guide and Artifacts announcement.

A fair 20-minute test

  1. Choose three tasks you genuinely do: one writing task, one research or reasoning task, and one file or data task.
  2. Use the same prompt, source material and success criteria in each product.
  3. Check factual accuracy against reliable sources, not just writing style.
  4. Record how much editing was needed and whether the tool handled follow-up instructions.
  5. Check plan limits, privacy controls, supported integrations and renewal price before paying.
Important: Never upload confidential employer, customer, financial or personal information unless you are authorised and have checked the service's data-handling terms.

Our practical recommendation

Choose by workflow, not brand. If you rely heavily on Google services, include Gemini in your test. If you need broad file, research and data workflows, test ChatGPT. If document drafting or file creation is central, include Claude. You may find one free option is enough; you do not need three subscriptions.

Before subscribing

  • Confirm which features are available in your country and on your account.
  • Compare current free and paid limits on the provider's own pricing page.
  • Check privacy, retention and training controls for your account type.
  • Re-test periodically because models and product features change.
