Contact ToolNest

Have a correction, a useful tool to suggest or a question about the website? Here's how to reach us.

Send a suggestion or correction

We welcome suggestions for useful tools, practical guide topics and corrections to information already published on ToolNest.

For now, the simplest way to contact the site is through the public GitHub repository for ToolNest:

Open ToolNest feedback and issues on GitHub ↗

What to include

  • The page or tool you are referring to.
  • What you think should be corrected or added.
  • A source link, if you are reporting an outdated feature, price or claim.

Privacy reminder

Please do not post passwords, payment details, private account information or other sensitive personal information in a public issue. GitHub issues are public and may be visible to anyone.

Partnership enquiries

If you represent a product or service and would like to suggest it for consideration, please use the same feedback channel and clearly identify the organisation you represent. Submission does not guarantee a listing, review or partnership.
